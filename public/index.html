How a meeting runs

  1. Brief check. The chair checks your brief has a decision, at least two options and your constraints. If something is missing it asks once, then works from assumptions it writes down.
  2. Round 1: verdicts. Each advisor sees only your brief and answers alone: a call, three reasons from its own frameworks, and one thing to watch out for.
  3. Round 2: debate. Each advisor reads all five verdicts with names attached and replies: who it agrees with, who is wrong and why, and whether it changed its mind.
  4. Round 3: minutes. The chair records the vote, where they clashed, the strongest dissent, a recommendation that respects your constraints, and the one question to answer before you decide.

You get an interactive report: a tab per verdict, the debate as cards, and the minutes with a copy button. It is private to you; share it with a link you can revoke. Meetings usually take 2 to 6 minutes, depending on how busy the network is.

Voice meetings (optional). Tick "Voice meeting" and the board meets on a live PairUX call you join from the PairUX desktop app or any browser. The chair calls each member in turn, six distinct voices, one at a time; ask a question by speaking or typing in the call chat and the chair hands it to the right member at the next pause. Your questions and the answers land in the report as "Founder Q&A". Calls are recorded for you only (encrypted, everyone on the call is told), and you can delete a recording at any time.

The board

The CFO

Cash, runway, unit economics and payback. Puts a number on everything.

The Growth Marketer

Audience, distribution and revenue growth. Finds the narrowest stage of the funnel.

The Operator

Systems, delegation readiness and execution. Documents before delegating.

The Founder-Time Strategist

Protects your hours and keeps you on the work only you can do.

The Contrarian Investor

Inversion, reversibility and opportunity cost. Looks for what everyone missed.

The Chair

Runs the meeting, quotes everyone fairly, never votes, and will recommend against the majority when the dissent is stronger.

API, CLI, MCP and TUI

Everything the page does, a script or an agent can do. Sign in with OAuth 2.1 from any app, or get an API key.

CLI and TUI

npm i -g @profullstack/board-saas
board-saas login        # OAuth: open the link, type the code
board-saas meet "Decision: ... Options: ... Constraints: ..." --data financials.txt
board-saas meet "..." --voice     # live call on PairUX; prints the join link
board-saas share <id> --recording --expires 7d
board-saas tui

HTTP API

curl -X POST https://board.saas.pit.world/api/v1/meetings \
  -H "Authorization: Bearer $TOKEN" \
  -H "content-type: application/json" \
  -d '{"brief":"Decision: ... Options: ... Constraints: ...","company_data":"cash $41k, MRR $6.2k, burn $5.9k/mo"}'
curl https://board.saas.pit.world/api/v1/meetings/<id> -H "Authorization: Bearer $TOKEN"
